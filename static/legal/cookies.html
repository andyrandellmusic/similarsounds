Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

The short version
  • SimilarSounds itself sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We save a few things on your own device so the site remembers your set - nothing is sent to us.
  • Players from Deezer, SoundCloud and YouTube can set their own cookies, so they only load after you choose.

1. What we store on your device

These use your browser's local storage (similar to cookies). They are strictly necessary for features you ask for, never leave your device, and we can't see them.

NameWhat it remembersHow long
ss_setThe tracks in "Your set" and their order.Until you clear it
ss_recentYour last 5-6 searches, shown when you tap the search box.Until you clear it
ss_plays, ss_reviewed, ss_review_atHow many previews you've played, and whether you've already rated us, so the "Give us a review" box only appears once (after 60 plays).Until you clear it
ss_langThe language you picked for the site.Until you clear it
ss_consentWhich music players you've chosen to allow (Deezer, SoundCloud, YouTube).Until you change or clear it

2. Third-party players (only if you choose)

To play music we embed players run by other companies. They may set their own cookies or similar technology, for example to remember settings or measure plays. That's why each one shows a short notice first and only loads when you choose Load player. Your choice is remembered for that player on this device.

PlayerWhen it loadsTheir cookie information
DeezerWhen you press play on a track preview.Deezer privacy & cookies
SoundCloudWhen you paste a SoundCloud link and load its player.SoundCloud Cookie Policy
YouTube (Google)When you paste a YouTube link and load its player.How Google uses cookies

Beatport: we only link to Beatport's website. Nothing from Beatport loads on SimilarSounds, so no Beatport cookies are set by visiting us.

Other content: cover art images load from Deezer's servers and fonts load from Google Fonts. These requests share your IP address with those companies but SimilarSounds does not ask them to set cookies. See our Privacy Policy.

3. Manage your choices

Checking your current choices…

You can also clear or block site data in your browser settings. Blocking it won't stop the site working, but your set and recent searches won't be remembered.

4. Changes

If we add or change anything we store, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: andyrandellmusic@gmail.com.
