Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version
  • No ads, no third-party analytics or tracking - and we never sell data. You never need an account; creating one is optional.
  • Your set and recent searches are saved only on your own device.
  • Our web host keeps short-lived server logs (including IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure.
  • Music players from SoundCloud, YouTube and Deezer only load when you choose, and those companies have their own policies.

1. Who we are

SimilarSounds (www.similarsounds.com) is a free house-music discovery website run by Andy Randell, an individual based in the United Kingdom. For this website, Andy Randell is the "controller" of your personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

Contact for anything privacy-related: management@similarsounds.com

2. What information we handle

Information sent automatically when you visit

Like every website, when your browser requests a page our server receives technical information: your IP address, the page or search requested, the time, and your browser/device type. This appears in server logs kept by our hosting provider, Render, for a short period.

What you search for

When you type an artist or track, or paste a YouTube, SoundCloud, Beatport, Bandcamp or Deezer link, it is sent to our server so we can find the track and work out its key, tempo and energy. Our server looks the track up with Deezer, YouTube, SoundCloud or Bandcamp from our server (for Bandcamp we only read the page title) - your IP address is not passed to them for this step. We keep a temporary, anonymous cache of analysed tracks (the music data, not who searched) so repeat searches are faster. It is cleared whenever the server restarts.

Information stored on your device (not sent to us)

Your saved set, your recent searches and your player choices are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. We cannot see them. See our Cookie Policy for the full list and how to clear them.

Counting free searches

Each device gets a few free searches. To count them, your browser stores a random device number (it isn't linked to you), and with each search we record that number, how many searches it has used and which tracks it searched in the last day (so repeating a search is free). We also record a one-way scrambled code made from your IP address - never the address itself - to limit free searches per network per day. These records are kept by Supabase in London and used only for counting free searches.

Visitor count. When you open the site, we also record that same random device number and the date, plus how many pages it opened that day and your browser's time zone (for example Europe/London, which tells us roughly which country you're in), so we can see how many people visit and from where. If you're signed in, your account also keeps the time zone it was first seen in. It isn't linked to your name, email or IP address, isn't shared with anyone, and only SimilarSounds can see the totals. We don't use Google Analytics or any other tracking service.

If you create an account (optional)

You can use SimilarSounds without an account. If you create one, we store your name (shown on the site when you're signed in; you can change it any time), your email address, a securely scrambled (hashed) version of your password - we never see or keep the password itself - and the set and recent searches you save, so they're available on any device you log in on. We also keep the dates you signed up and last logged in. Accounts are run for us by Supabase, with the data held in London. You can also sign in with Google: Google then shares only your name and email address with us (no password, contacts or other Google data). If you choose to turn on Face ID sign-in (a "passkey"), your phone or computer creates the passkey and keeps it, protected by your Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or device PIN. We never receive your face, fingerprint or any biometric data - only a public key that lets us check it's you. You can turn it off in your account at any time. We use your email only to confirm your account and to send password-reset emails you ask for - never for marketing.

You can delete your account at any time from the account screen (Account → Delete my account); this permanently removes your name, email, password and saved data.

Star reviews

After you've played 60 previews we may ask you to rate SimilarSounds from 1 to 5 stars. If you choose a rating, we save only the number of stars, the date and time, the site language and how many previews you'd played. No name, email, IP address or other identifier is saved with it. Reviews are kept in a private store on GitHub (run by GitHub, Inc.) that only we can see, so we can improve the site.

Shared links

If you use "Copy link" or "Share your set", the link contains the Deezer track numbers of those tracks. Anyone you send it to can see those tracks. It contains nothing else about you.

We do not ask for your name, email or any account details, and we do not knowingly collect special category data.

3. Why we use it (our lawful basis)

  • Running the site and keeping it secure (server logs, handling your searches): our legitimate interests in providing a working, secure service you have asked to use.
  • Loading third-party music players: your consent, which you give by choosing to load a player. You can withdraw it at any time on our Cookie Policy page.
  • Counting free searches: our legitimate interests in offering a fair free allowance and preventing abuse.
  • Star reviews: our legitimate interests in learning what visitors think of the site. Giving a rating is entirely optional.
  • Your account (if you create one): to provide the service you've asked for - keeping your saved set and logging you in (performance of a contract).
  • Replying to you if you email us: our legitimate interests in answering your message.

5. How long we keep it

  • Server logs: for a short period set by our host Render, then deleted.
  • Analysis cache: until the server restarts (usually within a day).
  • Your device's local storage: until you clear it - see the Cookie Policy.
  • Free-search counts: the network code for one day; device and account counts for as long as the free allowance applies (deleted with your account).
  • Accounts: until you delete your account. We may delete accounts that haven't been used for 3 years.
  • Star reviews: for as long as the site runs; they contain nothing that identifies you.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to deal with your message.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. If you have an account you can delete it yourself at any time; otherwise the main personal data is in short-lived server logs. Email us and we'll help with any request. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the UK's data regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

7. Children

SimilarSounds is not aimed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
