# Third-party brand assets

Only official files, used exactly as each company's guidelines describe.

## SoundCloud (in use)
- `soundcloud/logo_white.png` - downloaded from https://developers.soundcloud.com/docs/api/buttons-logos
- Rules followed: shown where SoundCloud content appears (the "From SoundCloud" card), links back to
  the work on soundcloud.com, credits the uploader, white logo on SoundCloud black #121212,
  unaltered and not below 30 px wide.

## YouTube (in use)
- `youtube/yt_logo_fullcolor_white_digital.png` - from the official logo pack downloaded from
  https://brand.youtube after the site owner accepted YouTube's brand terms (Digital / 01 Full Color).
- Rules followed (https://developers.google.com/youtube/branding): shown next to the YouTube content
  (the "From YouTube" card), clickable to that video, unaltered (full-colour red icon, white text for
  dark backgrounds, built-in clear space kept), on a solid background, never the most prominent
  element; the channel is credited and linked.

## Deezer, Beatport
- Not used: no public brand kit / permission yet. Text links only.
