Hello Deezer partnerships team,

My name is Andy Randell. I'm a DJ and producer in the UK, and I run SimilarSounds (www.similarsounds.com), a discovery tool that helps DJs find new house music that mixes well with a track they already love.

**How SimilarSounds uses Deezer today**

- A visitor picks or pastes a track. We return new house releases that sound similar and are compatible in key, tempo and energy, so they can be mixed together.
- Track details, artwork and 30-second previews come from the Deezer API (about 8,700 house tracks so far, plus new releases added daily).
- We analyse each 30-second preview once to estimate its musical key, BPM and energy. We keep only those measurements, never the audio itself.
- Previews play through the official Deezer widget, and every track is credited to Deezer and links back to it. SimilarSounds sends DJs to Deezer to listen in full.

**What I'd like to do**

I'd like to fund the site's running costs with an optional subscription: a few free searches, then £4.99 a month for unlimited searches. I understand the Deezer for Developers terms only allow non-commercial use. I haven't charged anyone, and I won't until I have your permission.

**My questions**

1. Could SimilarSounds get a commercial licence or partnership to use the Deezer API, previews and widget as described above? If so, what are the terms and costs?
2. If a subscription isn't possible, is there another model you'd accept, such as advertising, affiliate links to Deezer Premium, or a revenue share?
3. Is analysing previews to estimate key, BPM and energy (and storing only those numbers) acceptable to you?

I'm keen to do this properly and give Deezer clear credit and visibility. I'd be happy to show you the site, share usage figures, or put a "Listen on Deezer" or "Try Deezer Premium" link on every result.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.
